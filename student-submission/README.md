# My Final Project
## Project Name
Smart Assistant for Training Plan Organization.
## Idea Selected
Project Title:
Smart Assistant for Training Plan Organization
Project Idea:
The project is a simple AI assistant that helps employees or trainers create organized and personalized training plans based on their goals, current skill level, available time, and training period.
Goal:
The goal is to save time and effort when creating training plans and turn general learning goals into clear, practical, and measurable steps.
## Problem Statement
Employees and trainers may face difficulties such as:
Creating a clear and organized training plan.
Choosing the right skills to focus on.
Dividing training activities across days or weeks.
Tracking progress and identifying the next steps.
The AI assistant solves this problem by creating a simple, structured, and practical training plan.
## Target Users
Employees: People who want to improve their professional skills and productivity.
New Employees: Employees who need a structured training plan during onboarding.
Trainers: Professionals who need help creating organized training programs.
Managers and Team Leaders: People who want to support employee development and skill improvement.
Students and Interns: Individuals preparing for workplace tasks and professional skills.
## R-C-T-F Prompt
R – Role:
You are an AI assistant specialized in organizing training plans and improving productivity in the workplace.
C – Context:
The user will provide their training goal, current skill level, available training time, and the duration of the training plan.
T – Task:
Create a practical and organized training plan divided into days or weeks. For each period, specify the training topic, activity, suggested duration, and a way to measure progress.

Training Plan Assistant Prompt
You are an AI assistant specialized in organizing training plans and improving productivity in the workplace.
 The user will provide their training goal, current skill level, available training time, and desired training period.
 Create a practical and organized training plan based on the user's needs. Divide the plan into days or weeks and include the training topic, activity, suggested duration, and a way to measure progress for each period.
 Present the plan in a table with the following columns:
 Day/Week | Training Topic | Activity | Duration | Progress Measurement
 At the end, provide 2–3 short tips to help the user stay motivated and committed to the training plan.
 If important information is missing, ask the user for clarification instead of making assumptions.
## Sample Input
I want to improve my Excel skills.
My current level is beginner.
I can train for one hour per day, five days a week.
I want a two-week training plan.
## Sample Output
Here’s a practical 2-week Excel training plan designed for a beginner, with 1 hour per day, 5 days per week.
Day/Week	Training Topic	Activity	Duration	Progress Measurement
Day 1	Excel Basics	Learn the interface, workbooks, worksheets, cells, rows, columns, and basic data entry. Practice formatting cells.	60 min	Create a simple personal expense sheet with at least 15 entries.
Day 2	Formulas & Basic Functions	Practice +, -, *, /, SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, and MAX.	60 min	Build a small sales table and calculate totals and averages without assistance.
Day 3	Cell References & Formatting	Learn relative vs. absolute references, number formats, borders, alignment, and conditional formatting.	60 min	Format a sample budget and use conditional formatting to highlight values above a chosen amount.
Day 4	Sorting & Filtering	Practice sorting data, applying filters, and creating Excel Tables.	60 min	Take a dataset of 20+ records and successfully filter and sort it by multiple criteria.
Day 5	Week 1 Practice Project	Create a simple monthly budget or sales tracker using the skills from Days 1–4.	60 min	Complete the project independently and calculate at least 5 useful metrics.
Day 6	Logical Functions	Learn IF, AND, OR, and basic nested logic.	60 min	Create a worksheet that automatically labels results as "Pass/Fail" or "On Track/Behind."
Day 7	Lookup Functions	Learn XLOOKUP if available; otherwise practice VLOOKUP. Understand lookup values and ranges.	60 min	Create a product-price table and retrieve prices automatically using a product ID.
Day 8	Charts & Visualization	Create column, bar, line, and pie charts. Learn how to choose an appropriate chart for different data.	60 min	Turn your sales or budget data into at least 2 clear charts.
Day 9	PivotTables	Learn how to create a PivotTable, summarize data, group information, and change fields.	60 min	Create a PivotTable that summarizes sales by category and month.
Day 10	Final Project & Review	Build a small business-style Excel report combining formulas, formatting, filters, lookups, charts, and a PivotTable.	60 min	Complete the report independently and identify at least 3 insights from your data.
Motivation tips
Practice with realistic data: Use expenses, sales, work tasks, or another dataset relevant to your daily life.
Spend more time doing than watching: Aim for roughly 15 minutes learning and 45 minutes practicing each day.
Track your progress: At the end of each session, write down one Excel function or technique you can now use without help.
## Safety Checklist
1-Do not share passwords or confidential company information.
2- Protect personal and employee data.
3- Verify AI-generated information before using it.
4- Do not use the assistant to make sensitive decisions about employees.
5-Keep training recommendations appropriate and realistic.
6-Do not rely on AI as the only source for important workplace decisions.
7- Review and update the training plan when the user's needs change.
## Reflection
What did I learn?
I learned that AI can transform a general training goal into a clear and organized plan. I also learned that using the R-C-T-F framework helps create more precise and useful prompts.
What will I improve later?
In the future, I would improve the assistant by allowing it to create training plans based on the employee's job role, experience level, and required skills. I would also add a progress-tracking feature that can automatically adjust the training plan based on the user's performance.
